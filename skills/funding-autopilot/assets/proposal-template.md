### Problem, gap and related research

Describe the real unmet need, current state of the art and a clearly falsifiable research question.

### Specific aims, methodology and evaluation

State hypotheses, methodology, comparators, datasets or evidence sources, success/guardrail criteria, work packages, deliverables and budgeted resources.

### Work plan, milestones, risk and alternatives

Provide dated milestones and realistic stop/recovery decisions. Do not present planned experiments as completed.

### Applicant, PI/host and institutional fit

Use CV-grounded achievements and explicitly confirmed partner commitments.

### Impact, engagement, open science, ethics and data

Match the funder's notion of impact. Do not substitute publication count for adoption.

### Budget and justification

Use validated institutional costing. Show what pays the applicant, if anything.
