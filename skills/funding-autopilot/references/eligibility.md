# Eligibility, host and deadline decision contract

## No eligibility claim from topic matching

Each candidate has independent gates: (1) source currency and official call; (2) applicant role (individual PI, fellow, employee, co-lead, collaborator, student); (3) degree requirements measured at stated dates; (4) host/PI/institution approval; (5) geography/mobility/career window; (6) permitted research aims; (7) salary/budget; (8) external and internal deadline; (9) submission format and sign-off.

Label each gate: **pass / fail / unknown / not applicable** with a quoted official rule/section. Unknown **never** becomes pass because the applicant has a strong CV. Fatal fail => **ineligible**; past validated deadline => **expired**; missing mandatory official documents or stale round => **unverified**; verified call but unresolved applicant/host fact => **conditional**; all applicable verified constraints met => **eligible**, subject to human and institution checks.

## Degree, employment and role

"Expected to submit the thesis in November" is not "thesis submitted", "viva passed" or "degree conferred". Treat planned dates as plans; conditional eligibility may exist when the deadline is in the future and the rule explicitly permits it. Check whether the funder requires a PhD by application date, start date, or at formal award. Career clock may run from viva, award date or submission, and leave/extension rules differ.

A new PI/mentor can normally be approached independently of the doctoral supervisor, but cannot be named as confirmed without consent. A PI-led award may transfer resources to an institution, not to the applicant personally. Salary support, institutional employer, contract and visa/employment permissions remain separate. If the user wants to leave the original group, do not assume any institutional approval by the original group or that it is unnecessary; determine the new host's exact process.

## Deadlines and audit trail

Source both funder close and receiving institution/dept research-office close. Include date and local time plus timezone; convert to UTC only with a verified timezone. A funder deadline after the internal deadline does *not* reopen a closed internal submission route. For host-institution-specific calls, check new host's process rather than assuming Cambridge dates apply at Oxford, UCL or another university.

Do not recommend contacting an ineligible PI, invent eligibility exceptions or guarantee funding. For uncertain cases, suggest a short factual inquiry to the funder or new host's grants office with the unanswered question; do not send it without user approval.

## Two-path decision

When applicant wants to move:
- **Independent route:** applicant is eligible in own right as fellow/individual/lead; new hosting institution may still be required. Track whether the user can personally receive salary.
- **New PI route:** call permits the role and the chosen qualified PI agrees to lead; applicant's employment and funded time must be arranged separately. Role is conditional until confirmed.

Report both routes independently, not a blended probability or single "you can apply" claim. Scores describe research/program fit only and never overwrite eligibility classification.
